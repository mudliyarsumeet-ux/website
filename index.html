Exercise guide

Beginner-friendly movements you can practise at home.

🪑

Chair squat

Sit back toward a stable chair and stand tall again.

  • Keep feet stable
  • Move slowly
  • Use the chair for confidence
🤲

Wall push-up

Hands on a wall, body straight, bend and press back.

  • Keep your body aligned
  • Control both directions
  • Step farther back to progress
🌉

Glute bridge

Lie on your back with knees bent and gently lift your hips.

  • Keep feet planted
  • Pause briefly at the top
  • Avoid over-arching your back
🦵

Supported reverse lunge

Hold a stable support and step one foot back before returning.

  • Use support for balance
  • Keep the front foot stable
  • Move only as far as comfortable
🐦

Bird dog

From hands and knees, extend opposite arm and leg with control.

  • Keep your trunk steady
  • Move slowly
  • Alternate sides
⏱️

Plank

Hold a comfortable plank position while keeping your body controlled.

  • Start with a short hold
  • Keep breathing
  • Use an easier version if needed

Rest timer

Use this between sets so you don't have to keep checking the clock.

How to use it

For most beginner strength exercises, start around 60–90 seconds between sets. Take a little longer if your breathing has not settled or you cannot perform the next set with control.

This timer is a simple training aid, not a medical device.
